Corporate
Journey

Expanding beyond advisory into principal investment

Since 2019, Affirmate Group has evolved from a business advisory firm into an integrated business and investment platform.

  1. 2019
    Founded

    ABN and APN established. Exclusive advisor for the Menteng Eksekutif (Agung Podomoro) transaction.

  2. 2020
    Expansion

    Supported PT Tatamulia's third-party share allotment. Established PT Ritz Affirmate Internasional (JV).

  3. 2022
    Development

    Strategic advisory for the Toda Corp x Ciputra high-rise project (about IDR 1.5T).

  4. 2023
    Admin Platform

    ATN established. Launched the administrative platform: work permit, licensing and corporate support services.

  5. 2024
    Alliance

    Partnership with The 77 Bank. Began hosting its expatriate officer in Indonesia.

  6. 2025
    Major Deal

    Lead advisor for the NTT East x SURGE strategic investment (about IDR 4T).

  7. 2026
    Evolution

    Acquired a 5.6% stake in PT BOT Finance (member of MUFG). BARTIGA / JTC launched.
